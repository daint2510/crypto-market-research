# Crypto Market Research

## About

This project is a simple crypto market research tool.

It focuses on:

- Cryptocurrency market trends
- Token prices
- Trading volume
- Technical indicators
- Market opportunities
- Basic on-chain research

## Goal

The goal is to make crypto market information easier to understand.

The project collects useful market data and turns it into simple summaries.

## Features

- Real-time market data
- Price monitoring
- Technical analysis
- Market trend summaries
- Simple research reports

## Example Research

A typical research report may include:

1. Current market trend
2. Price movement
3. Trading volume
4. Technical indicators
5. Important market events

## Future Plans

- Add more market data sources
- Improve technical analysis
- Add automated reports
- Support more blockchain networks

## Disclaimer

This project is for research and educational purposes only.

It is not financial advice.
